# Draft Commercial EULA Outline

Status: business/legal drafting outline only. Not approved terms.

## Parties

- Licensor: to be filled by legal.
- Customer/licensee: the person or entity accepting the paid commercial terms.

## License Grant

Draft concept: a paid, non-exclusive, non-transferable commercial license to use
arch-line-weights for production architectural drawing workflows beyond the
free-trial scope.

## Scope To Decide

- Seats, devices, firm-wide use, contractors, and student/studio use.
- Whether generated drawing outputs can be used commercially without further
  restriction.
- Whether internal modifications are allowed.
- Whether redistribution, SaaS hosting, resale, sublicensing, or embedding in
  another product is allowed.
- Support, updates, version eligibility, and renewal terms.

## Customer Duties

- Keep license keys, private drawings, and client materials confidential.
- Do not remove notices.
- Do not use the product to process assets the customer lacks rights to use.
- Follow payment, export-control, and applicable law requirements.

## Disclaimers And Limits

To be drafted by legal: warranty disclaimer, limitation of liability,
indemnity, termination, governing law, venue, and survival clauses.

## Data And Privacy

To be drafted by legal: whether the CLI is local-only, when optional AI or cloud
services may receive data, and what user consent or data-processing terms apply.

## Release Gate

This EULA outline can support #4 planning, but #4 should remain open until an
approved final EULA and license swap are merged.
