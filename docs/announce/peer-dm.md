Goal: get **5 named USC peers to run it on ONE board each.** Send 1:1, not a broadcast.
Lead with *their* pain (hand-fixing strokes in Illustrator), not the tech. Make the ask tiny.

> export (or screen-share) and I'll show you the before/after live. If it saves you time, a ⭐ and

## Rules of engagement
- **5 named people, 1:1.** A drop in a group chat converts ~0.
- **Tiny ask:** one board, ~2 min, you'll help. Not "check out my project."
- **Every reply becomes a GitHub issue or a testimonial** — that's the traction the launch didn't get.
- Track who you messaged + their response in `SHIP_CHECKLIST.md` so it doesn't evaporate.
